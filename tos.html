What your key gives you

A key is a personal, non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence to run one client on one machine for the period stated on the plan you bought. You are renting access for a period. You are not buying the software, and nothing here transfers ownership of it.

A key covers a single game. A key for one client will not run another, so using two games means buying two keys.

The licence period starts at first launch, not at purchase, and runs continuously from that moment. It does not pause while you are not playing.

On first launch the key binds to a hardware identifier taken from your computer. It will not run elsewhere while bound.

  • 30-day keys: unlimited resets, subject to fair use.

Ask in the support channel to use a reset. Repeated reset requests that look like key sharing will be refused, and continued attempts end the licence under section 12.

Keys are issued per client. A game we release later is a separate client with its own keys, prices and status row, and buying one does not give you access to it.
