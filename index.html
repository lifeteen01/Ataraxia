What you are actually buying

Rust and Fortnite both run kernel-level anti-cheat, and cheating in either can get your account banned without warning. We will not tell you Ataraxia is undetectable, because no company selling this can know that. We publish what we currently see, we stop selling when we see trouble, and we expect you to use an account you can afford to lose.

Every client we sell breaks the rules of the game it is built for. That is your decision to make, not ours to hide. The terms spell out where our responsibility ends.

Questions

Will I get banned?

Possibly. EAC and BattlEye both detect cheats, sometimes weeks after a build ships, and neither game warns you first. The status board is the honest version of this answer: it says what we know today and nothing more.

Does one key cover both games?

No. Each game is a separate client with its own builds, so it needs its own key. Two games is two purchases and two keys, and that will stay true of everything we add later.

What happens when a client goes down?

Sales for that game stop within the hour, its row on the status board changes state, and every day of downtime is credited back to active keys when the next working build ships. Other clients keep running unless their own rows say otherwise.

Can I use one key on two computers?

No. A key locks to one machine the first time it launches. Seven-day keys include one reset and 30-day keys are unlimited. Ask in the support channel and the lock gets cleared.

Which payment methods work?

Card and crypto, through our checkout provider. Card details never reach us. PayPal is not supported and never will be.

Is there a free trial?

No. The 24-hour key exists for that reason.

How do I get support?

Start in the community room for your game, where most answers already exist. Key, payment and billing problems go to support@ataraxia.example, answered between 09:00 and 01:00 UTC.
